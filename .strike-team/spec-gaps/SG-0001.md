# SG-0001 — Scenario minimum for narrative specified facets

## Discovered in
Phase: P001 planning (affected implementation is later validation and scenario coverage)
Team: Codex
Agent: spec-analyst; adjudicated by Implementation Lead

## Relevant spec sections
Core Specification v2.1 §7.3 R3, §26.2, §26.3, §12.4.

## Missing semantic decision
§7.3 R3 says every active specified facet SHALL have at least `governance.min_scenarios_per_rule` scenarios exercising it. §26.2 limits that minimum to evaluable specified facets. §26.3 allows narrative scenarios as examples, requires `verification.mode`, and says they SHALL NOT raise confidence automatically. The frozen text does not say whether an active narrative specified rule must meet the count.

## Why implementation cannot proceed safely
Enforcing R3 literally rejects a narrative rule with no scenarios; enforcing §26.2 literally accepts it. A future `kf validate` or coverage implementation cannot choose one without changing normative behavior.

## Concrete alternatives discovered
- Apply the minimum to every active specified rule, including narrative rules, while preserving the no-automatic-confidence rule.
- Apply the minimum only to evaluable specified rules; narrative rules rely on `verification.mode`.

## Affected workstreams
P007 full semantic validation and P014 scenario coverage/checking. P001 only models the narrative `verification.mode` shape and does not implement scenario count checks.

## Unaffected work that may continue
P001 schemas, repository foundation, and P001 fixture; P002–P006 work that does not enforce the conflicting count.

## Required decision authority
User or future frozen-spec revision.
