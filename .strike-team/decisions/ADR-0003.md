# ADR-0003 — Roadmap as a DAG with a CLI backbone

**Status**: Accepted 2026-10-09

## Context
Phases P001–P027 were strictly sequential ("previous phase accepted"). That serialized independent work, and no phase delivered the §36 CLI contract or the §55 conformance runner early, so progress could not be measured against the spec's operational definition of done.

## Decision
- P001–P027 keep their IDs. Dependencies become "needs the artifact of phase X", not "phase X accepted".
- New phase P028, "CLI Backbone and Conformance Runner": `bin/kf`, the §36 JSON envelope and exit codes (§36.1), schema-only `kf validate`, a runner for `conformance/expected/`, and CI. It runs right after P001, in parallel with P002.
- The pure-logic track P011 → P012 → P013 may start depending only on P001.
- Progress is measured by the §55 `expected/` scoreboard and the §56 Milestone 1 items, not by the number of phases accepted.

## Consequences
- More parallelism, with a dependency graph that must be maintained in `PHASES.md`.
- Each phase integrates through the CLI backbone early, exposing cross-phase mismatches sooner.
- "Done" becomes operational (§55: the fixture is the operational definition of "terminado"), reducing subjective acceptance.
