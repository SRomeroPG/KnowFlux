# ADR-0004 — Audit rubric

**Status**: Accepted 2026-10-09

## Context
Prior audit rounds accumulated findings of mixed weight, and re-audits could open a new full round for a small regression, delaying acceptance without improving conformance.

## Decision
- A finding is REQUIRED only if it cites (a) a violated SHALL / SHALL NOT of the frozen spec, (b) a binding errata entry, or (c) a failing §55 `expected/` line. Everything else is ADVISORY and never blocks acceptance.
- A single LOW regression found on re-audit is fixed and verified within the same round; it does not open a new full round.
- Opus is used only for adjudication of CRITICAL phases. Sonnet is the default auditor; Haiku handles mechanical enumeration and command runs.
- Severity scale stays CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW. Prior findings are re-verified independently on re-audit.

## Consequences
- Acceptance depends on traceable authority, not auditor preference.
- Advisory observations are still reported but cannot extend a phase.
- Regressions above LOW found on re-audit still require a new implementation round.
