# ADR-0002 — Spec errata mechanism and decision authority

**Status**: Accepted 2026-10-09

## Context
The spec is frozen and contains underspecified points (SG-0001..SG-0005) and textual defects. Under the old rule any gap blocked work until the human or a spec revision decided, which stalled P002 and P007 planning.

## Decision
- A normative-additional document, `knowledge-framework-spec-v2.1-errata.md`, holds binding decisions. It never overrides an explicit SHALL / SHALL NOT and never edits the spec.
- The Foreman may decide any spec gap. Each decision records options, rationale, reversibility (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) and affected phases. It prefers the option most consistent with the frozen text, most reversible, and most conservative (reject/manual over silent auto-resolution).
- The human ratifies asynchronously: `DECIDED_BY_FOREMAN` → `RATIFIED` or `REJECTED`. A rejected decision becomes a remediation task.
- `BLOCKED_BY_SPEC_GAP` is replaced by `BLOCKED_BY_DECISION`; the Foreman exits it by writing an errata entry (or marking it `OPEN` with a recommendation).
- `decisions/` (ADRs) is for process decisions; the errata is for semantics.

## Consequences
- Work resumes without waiting on the human, at the cost of possible rework when a decision is rejected; recorded reversibility bounds that cost.
- HIGH-reversibility entries (persistent identity or format) stay `OPEN` until decided deliberately, and block PLAN approval of their phases.
- Audits cite binding errata entries as authority alongside spec clauses.
